# Hipparcos bright-star subset
Source: ESA (1997), The Hipparcos and Tycho Catalogues, I/239/hip_main,
served by CDS VizieR: https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/I/239
Query retrieved 2026-09-07: HIP, RAICRS, DEICRS, Vmag with Vmag < 6.5.
Coordinates are ICRS at catalog epoch J1991.25, in degrees. Proper motion,
aberration, and epoch propagation are NOT applied: this is a research baseline,
not a current-epoch flight catalog. See the source catalog for uncertainties,
double-star flags and other quality filters needed for a production catalog.
VizieR terms: https://cds.unistra.fr/vizier-org/licences_vizier.html

Included stars: 8785.
